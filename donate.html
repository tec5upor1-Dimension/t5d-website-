FAQ

Common questions.

Is this the same as Community Funding?

No. This is a direct, voluntary donation with no token or entitlement attached. Community Funding is a separate process, described on the Community Funding page.

Which assets can I send?

Any native coin or token on an EVM-compatible network — Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, BNB Chain, Avalanche C-Chain, and similar. See the network list above.

What if I send a non-EVM asset by mistake?

It cannot be recovered. This address only exists on EVM-compatible networks; a non-EVM transfer (Bitcoin, Solana, XRP, etc.) has nowhere to arrive and is lost.

Will I get anything back?

No. Donations are voluntary contributions with no expectation of a product, token, or refund of any kind.
