Funding Milestones

Two plain thresholds, not fine print.

Rather than technical "soft cap / hard cap" language, Community Funding is planned around two simple milestones. Both figures below are current internal planning references, not yet active or published terms.

PLANNED

Milestone 1 — Funding Secured

$10,000. The minimum Community Funding needs to raise to move the project forward — covering initial equipment and operating runway. If the window closes before this is reached, every contribution is refunded automatically; nothing is kept.

PLANNED

Milestone 2 — Community Funding Complete

$700,000. The full Community Funding allocation (70,000,000 T5D at the planned $0.01 price) fully subscribed. Community Funding can also close early if this is reached before the window ends.

Refund promise: contributions are only ever kept by the project once Milestone 1 is reached. Below that, refunds are automatic and self-serve — no request needed.

Community Allocation Reference

Participation designed to stay community-first.

The current plan sets aside 7% for Community Funding and a separate 5% for public sale / liquidity. These are planning references, not active terms or a promise of entitlement.

The T5D Base token is capped at 1,000,000,000. Rather than minting the full cap on day one, each allocation (Community Funding, team, strategic, and any future rounds) is minted only when it's actually needed; once every planned allocation is minted, a one-way switch permanently disables further minting.

FAQ

Current answers, stated plainly.

Is Community Funding active?

Not yet — but T5D keeps growing, and Community Funding is close. The official wallet, route, terms, and price will all appear here together the moment it opens.

Which assets are planned?

ETH, USDC, and USDT are the planned payment assets, with ETH and USDT auto-converted to USDC in-app before contribution. Exact routes and verification details remain unpublished.

Can supply ever exceed the cap?

No. Total supply is hard-capped at 1,000,000,000 T5D on-chain. Allocations are minted only as they're needed rather than all at once, and minting can be permanently disabled once every planned allocation is complete.

What is the intended official ticker?

T5D. The intended identity is separate from older test assets that used the same ticker.

What is the official contract address?

Not published yet. The official Base contract will be posted here with the activation details so the community has one clear verification source.

When will terms be available?

In preparation. Price, limits, delivery, eligibility, custody, and timing will be shared here with the official activation notice.
