FAQ

Current answers, stated plainly.

Is the T5D presale active?

No. This page is a pre-activation interface. It does not connect wallets, collect data, accept funds, or generate a payment route.

Which assets are planned?

SOL, ETH, USDC, USDT, BTC, BNB, and XRP are the requested major-asset set. Exact native routes and verification details remain unpublished.

Will supply increase later?

No. The intended Base token uses a fixed-supply model created once at deployment, without post-deployment minting.

What is the intended official ticker?

T5D. The intended identity is separate from older test assets that used the same ticker.

What is the official contract address?

Not published. A ticker is not a contract address and is never a payment or trading instruction.

When will terms be available?

Not published. Price, limits, delivery, eligibility, custody, and timing will be stated here in a dated official activation notice before any buyer action.
